## Featured Private Project

### [StellarSpeak](https://www.stellarspeak.online) *(Private Repository)*
> **Web App:** [https://www.stellarspeak.online](https://www.stellarspeak.online)  
> **Android App (Google Play):** [Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=online.stellarspeak.www.twa)

An AI-powered interactive English learning platform (Web PWA & Android app) designed to guide learners from beginner to advanced levels (**A1 to C1**):
- **24/7 AI Smart Tutor** that identifies individual weak spots and gives instant feedback.
- **Automatic Placement Test** to match learners with the right starting level.
- **Pronunciation & Writing Coaches** for real-time speaking evaluation and instant writing corrections.
- **Interactive Role-Play Scenarios** simulating real-life conversations (job interviews, travel, dining, and daily life).
- **Leveled Content Library** with reading articles, audio stories, and offline-friendly support.
